Support

Need help, found a bug, or want to join the closed test? Email info@wraplet.app. Please include your phone model and, if possible, your Player ID (Settings).

Quick answers

Voice chat doesn't work? Allow the microphone (Android Settings > Apps > ODDCITY > Permissions), join an online match (Quick Match or Private Room) and stand near the other player. For distance talk use Phone > Contacts > Call.

Can't find a match? Quick Match opens a new city if none is open; invite a friend with a Private Room code.

Lost progress after reinstalling? Guest progress lives on that phone only. Sign in with Google or a username (Settings > Account) to keep your progress on any phone.
