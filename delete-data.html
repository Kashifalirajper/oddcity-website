Delete my ODDCITY data

ODDCITY by Wraplet · Android package com.wraplet.oddcity

How to request deletion

  1. Open ODDCITY > Settings and note your Player ID (shown under your profile / settings).
  2. Email info@wraplet.app with the subject "ODDCITY data deletion" and include your Player ID.
  3. We delete your account and data within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your guest account (Unity Authentication player ID)
  • Profile, display name, character look, progress, Sparks, cosmetics, home items, achievements, friends and blocked lists (Unity Cloud Save)

What may be kept

  • Anonymous, aggregated statistics that cannot identify you.
  • Records our advertising and analytics providers keep under their own policies (you can reset your advertising ID in Android Settings).

Uninstalling the app removes the data stored on your device.
