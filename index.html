How to play

One match. One clock.

Matches last 6 to 12 minutes. You pick the length, how tricky the secrets are, and how many bots fill the streets.

  1. 01

    Get your secrets

    Two or three missions, each with a how-to. Don't like one? Swap it for free in the first minute.

    LIVE GAMEPLAY
  2. 02

    Do them your way

    Walk, drive, take a job, make a deal, start trouble. Stuck? Tap WHAT NOW? and follow the yellow path.

    LIVE GAMEPLAY
  3. 03

    The reveal

    When the clock hits zero, every secret is turned over. Most Fame wins — and climbs the Top Players board.

    LIVE GAMEPLAY
